Artist

About

I'm Marco Maraggi — comic book artist and illustrator working under the alias LIMBO, based in Italy. I graduated in Sequential Art from the International School of Comics in 2018.

My work spans graphic novels, illustrated biographies, album artwork, and editorial illustration. I'm particularly drawn to science fiction, cyberpunk, and psychedelic aesthetics — building immersive color worlds and narrative atmospheres that sit somewhere between the futuristic and the surreal.

I've worked on graphic biographies of Banksy, Andy Warhol, and Harry Styles. I illustrated a comic book manual by Balthazar Pagani, co-illustrated with Otto Gabos. In 2023, one of my illustrations was exhibited in New York City at the SuperRare NYC gallery. I've collaborated with Tank Magazine (UK), and my books have been published across Italy, Poland, France, England, the United States, the Czech Republic, and Spain.

Beyond my own projects, I'm available as a colorist and inker for comics and sequential art projects.
